## 3. Laboratory Experimental Fabrication Schedule

### 1. Melting & Ingot Casting: Vacuum Induction Melting (VIM) + Vacuum Arc Remelting (VAR)
- **Parameters:** Base charge Fe-Ni-Cr-Mo. Target gas limits: [O] < 15 ppm, [N] < 25 ppm, [H] < 1.5 ppm, S < 0.003%, P < 0.005%.
- **Metallurgical Objective:** Ultra-clean steel practice to prevent non-metallic inclusions and promote high fracture toughness.

### 2. Homogenization Soak: High-temperature inert atmosphere soaking
- **Parameters:** 1200°C ± 10°C for 8.0 hours; furnace cool to 1050°C.
- **Metallurgical Objective:** Dissolve Cu-rich interdendritic segregation (Cu=2.41%) and homogenize Mo/Cr distributions.

### 3. Thermomechanical Forging: Hot forging / rolling to naval plate geometry
- **Parameters:** Breakdown forging 1150°C -> finish rolling at 920°C. Minimum total reduction ratio >= 4:1. Air cool to RT.
- **Metallurgical Objective:** Refine prior austenite grain size and break up casting dendrites.

### 4. Austenitization & Quench: Solutionizing & martensitic transformation
- **Parameters:** 910°C ± 5°C for 1.0 hour; rapid agitation water quench. Optional sub-zero bath (-73°C) for 1.0 hour.
- **Metallurgical Objective:** Complete lath martensitic transformation matrix with high dislocation density and supersaturated Cu/solutes.

### 5. Isochronal Tempering: Secondary hardening precipitation hold
- **Parameters:** 509°C ± 3°C for 1.0 hours; still air cool to room temperature.
- **Metallurgical Objective:** Co-precipitation of bcc/9R Cu nanoclusters and M2C secondary alloy carbides; nucleate fine retained austenite films.
