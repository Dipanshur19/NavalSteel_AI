MILITARY SPECIFICATION: MIL-S-24645A (SH)
STEEL PLATE, SHEET, OR COIL, AGE-HARDENING ALLOY, STRUCTURAL, HIGH STRENGTH (HSLA-100)

1. SCOPE
This specification covers high-strength low-alloy (HSLA) steel plates for naval surface ship and submarine hull construction.

2. CHEMICAL COMPOSITION (wt%)
The chemical composition of HSLA-100 plate steel shall conform to the following ladle analysis limits:
Carbon (C): 0.04 wt%
Manganese (Mn): 0.85 wt%
Phosphorus (P): 0.008 wt%
Sulfur (S): 0.002 wt%
Silicon (Si): 0.25 wt%
Nickel (Ni): 3.50 wt%
Chromium (Cr): 0.60 wt%
Molybdenum (Mo): 0.55 wt%
Copper (Cu): 1.60 wt%
Columbium/Niobium (Nb): 0.03 wt%
Iron (Fe): Balance

3. HEAT TREATMENT SCHEDULE
Austenitizing: Austenitized at 900 °C for 1.5 h, followed by water quench.
Aging / Precipitation Tempering: Tempered at 650 °C for 2.0 h, air cooled.

4. MECHANICAL PROPERTIES REQUIREMENTS
Yield Strength (0.2% offset): 690 MPa (100 ksi) minimum
Tensile Strength (UTS): 795 MPa (115 ksi) minimum
Charpy V-Notch Impact Toughness at -40°C: 80 J (60 ft-lb) minimum
Elongation in 50 mm: 18 % minimum
Rockwell Hardness: 28 HRC

5. WELDABILITY AND CRACKING SUSCEPTIBILITY
Maximum cold cracking parameter Pcm: 0.24.
No preheat required for plate thicknesses under 25 mm.
