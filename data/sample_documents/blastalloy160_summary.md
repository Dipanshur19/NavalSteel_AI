# BlastAlloy160: Systems Design of Transformation-Toughened Naval Hull Steel
**Author:** Arup Saha, Prof. Gregory B. Olson  
**Institution:** Northwestern University (Materials Science and Engineering)

## Alloy Chemistry (wt%)
The optimal Concept-B alloy formulation utilizes dual precipitation strengthening with retained austenite transformation toughening:

| Element | Weight Percent (wt%) | Status | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **C** | 0.05 | Measured | Low carbon matrix to suppress cold cracking |
| **Ni** | 6.50 | Measured | Stabilizes dispersed austenite for TRIP effect |
| **Cu** | 3.65 | Measured | BCC coherent nano-precipitates (Orowan bowing) |
| **Cr** | 1.84 | Measured | Partitions Cu and provides secondary M2C carbides |
| **Mo** | 0.60 | Measured | Primary M2C carbide former |
| **V** | 0.10 | Measured | Refines grain boundaries and pins precipitates |
| **Fe** | Balance | Balance | Bainitic/Martensitic parent matrix |

## Thermal Processing
- **Solution Treatment:** Austenitized at 900 °C for 1 hour, followed by water quench to room temperature and liquid nitrogen cooling.
- **Secondary Hardening Temper:** Tempered at 480 °C for 4.0 h.

## Measured Mechanical Properties
- **Yield Strength (YS):** 1100 MPa (160 ksi)
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 1260 MPa (183 ksi)
- **Charpy V-Notch Toughness (-40 °C):** 122 J (90 ft-lb)
- **Room Temperature Toughness (25 °C):** 176 J (130 ft-lb)
- **Elongation:** 16.5 %
- **Calculated Pcm:** 0.231 (Excellent field weldability without preheat)
